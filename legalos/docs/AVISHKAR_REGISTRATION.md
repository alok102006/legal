## Abstract (199 words, rulebook limit is 200)

Indian small and medium enterprises sign contracts, pay suppliers and answer legal notices without a lawyer in the room, and existing chatbots answer confidently even when wrong. LegalOS is a web-based legal and compliance system in which every answer must show its evidence or admit that it has none. It has five workspaces: contract review, vendor verification, notice response, compliance planning and a document knowledge base. A contract is split into clauses and checked against 24 rules grounded in Indian statutes across 29 contract types; a finding is shown only when the quoted clause is located word for word in the document. Vendor identifiers such as GSTIN and PAN are validated structurally and then confirmed against the live government registry. Legal notices in English, Hindi, Marathi and Gujarati are classified into 45 types, the response deadline is computed from stated, statutory and hearing dates, and a reply draft leaves blanks for a lawyer. Uploaded documents are searched with hybrid keyword and vector retrieval and every cited passage is re-verified before display. On a labelled set of 33 questions the correct passage ranks first for 32, and 604 automated tests cover the pipelines. The system supports decisions; it does not replace legal advice.

## Keywords (if asked)

Legal technology, retrieval-augmented generation, citation verification, Indian contract law, GST compliance, legal notices, SME, natural language processing, Hindi and Marathi NLP.

## Objectives (if asked, 4 lines)

1. Give an SME owner a verdict on a contract, supplier, notice or filing with the evidence shown beside it.
2. Make every AI statement verifiable: a quote found in the document, a section of law, or a government registry reply.
3. Handle Indian languages and formats: Hindi, Marathi, Gujarati notices; Indian date formats; GSTIN, PAN, TAN, Udyam, CIN.
4. Measure accuracy and show failures as failures instead of silent defaults.

## Methodology (if asked, short)

FastAPI backend with PostgreSQL and Qdrant; Gemini models used only for drafting and classification, never as the source of truth. Quote verification locates every cited span in the original text before a finding is shown. A deterministic rule engine (YAML and Python) holds the 24 contract rules and 30 compliance rules, each tagged with its statutory source and checked by a lint test. Live GSTIN verification through a government-registry API with caching. Document retrieval combines BM25 keyword search, vector search, exact-token matching and a reranker, with document routing. Evaluation: a 33-question labelled retrieval set (hit@1 96 percent, hit@8 100 percent) and 604 automated tests.

## Findings / outcome (if asked, short)

Working system with five workspaces demonstrated on a synthetic company (Kulkarni Textiles, Surat). Correct passage ranked first for 32 of 33 questions. Contract findings only appear when the clause is located word for word, so the system reports "not found" rather than inventing a clause. Deadline engine picks the earliest of stated, statutory and hearing dates and explains which one it used. All demo data is synthetic; no real company or person is used.
