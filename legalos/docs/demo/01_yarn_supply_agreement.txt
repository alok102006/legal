YARN SUPPLY AGREEMENT

This Yarn Supply Agreement ("Agreement") is made at Surat on 1st April 2026 between:

Kulkarni Textiles Private Limited, a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013, having its registered office at Plot 14, Sachin GIDC, Surat, Gujarat 394230 (the "Buyer");

AND

Patel Yarn Traders, a partnership firm having its office at Ring Road, Surat, Gujarat 395002 (the "Supplier").

1. SUPPLY
1.1 The Supplier shall supply polyester and viscose yarn of the grades listed in Schedule A to the Buyer against purchase orders issued from time to time.
1.2 The Buyer shall purchase a minimum quantity of 50,000 kg of yarn in each contract year ("Minimum Commitment").

2. PRICE AND PAYMENT
2.1 Prices shall be as stated in Schedule A. The Supplier may revise prices at any time by giving seven (7) days' written notice to the Buyer.
2.2 The Buyer shall pay each invoice within sixty (60) days of the date of the invoice.
2.3 Any delay in payment shall carry interest at 24% per annum, compounded monthly.

3. SHORTFALL CHARGE
3.1 If the Buyer purchases less than the Minimum Commitment in any contract year, the Buyer shall pay the Supplier a sum of Rs. 2,00,000/- (Rupees Two Lakh only) as agreed compensation for such shortfall, without any proof of loss.

4. DELIVERY AND QUALITY
4.1 The Supplier shall endeavour to deliver the yarn within thirty (30) days of each purchase order. Time shall not be of the essence.
4.2 Complaints about quality must be made within forty-eight (48) hours of delivery, failing which the goods shall be deemed accepted.

5. TERM AND TERMINATION
5.1 This Agreement shall remain in force for three (3) years from 1st April 2026 and shall renew automatically for successive periods of three (3) years unless either party gives ninety (90) days' written notice before the end of the then-current term.
5.2 The Supplier may terminate this Agreement at any time by giving fifteen (15) days' written notice. The Buyer may terminate only for the Supplier's uncured material breach.

6. LIABILITY AND INDEMNITY
6.1 The Buyer shall indemnify the Supplier against all losses, claims, damages and expenses of whatever nature arising out of or in connection with this Agreement, without any limit.

7. CONFIDENTIALITY
7.1 Each party shall keep the terms of this Agreement and all pricing confidential.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND DISPUTES
8.1 This Agreement shall be governed by the laws of India.
8.2 The courts at Mumbai shall have exclusive jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of this Agreement.

9. GENERAL
9.1 This Agreement is the entire agreement between the parties. Any amendment must be in writing and signed by both parties.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have executed this Agreement on the date first written above.
